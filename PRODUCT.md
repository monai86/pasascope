# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Thai speech-language therapists (SLPs), clinical educators, and speech-language pathology researchers working with Thai- and English-speaking children (primarily ages 2;6 to 7 years). They work in hospitals, clinics, and university settings on macOS (Apple silicon) and Windows laptops, often offline or under strict patient-privacy expectations.

## Product Purpose

PasaScope is a local, privacy-preserving desktop workspace that helps therapists turn a recorded therapy session into an attested CHAT transcript, CLAN-consistent descriptive language-sample measures (MLU-w, NDW, MATTR, VOCD-D, turn-taking, echolalia review cues), and a signed A4 clinical summary report—without recordings ever leaving their computer. The download site (`site/`) serves as the clear, trustworthy front door for therapists to understand what the tool does, inspect its clinical boundaries, and download the latest release.

## Brand Personality

- **Calm** — unhurried whitespace, balanced proportions, zero visual hype
- **Clinical** — honest about measurement rules, limitations, and human-in-the-loop review
- **Precise** — sharp typography, tabular numerals, hairline dividers, signal-to-text clarity

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS marketing templates (cookie-cutter 3-column icon cards, gradient hero text, inflated buzzwords, artificial urgency)
- Over-decorated AI landing pages (glowing glassmorphism blobs, diagonal stripe fills, excessive motion)
- Diagnostic or medical-device overclaiming (never imply automated ASD diagnosis or clinical norm validation)

## Design Principles

1. **Show the real instrument, not marketing fluff.** Let the live signal-to-text sample, real desktop screenshots, and exact CLAN formulas explain the product.
2. **Therapist authority comes first.** Every visual and copy choice reinforces that AI drafts and counts, while the therapist verifies, attests, and interprets.
3. **Privacy and boundaries are prominent, not hidden in fine print.** Offline-only processing and non-diagnostic limits belong in plain sight.
4. **Minimalism with bilingual craft.** Thai (`Prompt` / `Noto Sans Thai`) and English must read cleanly side by side via an instant toggle, with generous line-height for Thai script.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA contrast minimum (≥ 4.5:1 for normal text, ≥ 3:1 for large headings) across both light and obsidian surfaces.
- Full keyboard navigation, visible focus rings, skip links, and semantic landmarks.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` so all content is immediately complete and legible without animation.
- Self-hosted fonts and zero external tracking scripts.
